Simulation Dynamics

The rocking-block contact model

Booster touchdowns are not binary collision boxes. Kinetic impact absorption and rotational stability are governed by separate physical constraints.

Figure 1: Stability envelope θ_tip = 25.6°

Impact restitution (v ≤ 63.0 m/s)

Touchdown energy is not a binary failure. Impacts below 63 m/s reflect with vertical damping coefficient e = 0.4 and friction, reproducing realistic rebounds before the vehicle settles flat.

Geometric stability limit (θ_tip = 25.6°)

Derived directly from the landing gear baseline (22m footprint span, 23m center-of-mass height). Past 25.6°, the center of mass falls outside the footprint polygon, causing an irreversible topple under gravity.

Dynamic foot pivoting & RCS authority

When one leg contacts first, the booster pivots on that foot under gravity torque. The agent's side RCS thrusters retain 25% control authority to save marginal tips before the settling timer resolves.

Formulations

Guidance & policy mathematics

Analytical equations governing suicide burn ignition triggers, continuous actor-critic optimization, and potential reward shaping.

01 • Suicide burn trigger altitude
h_burn = (v_y)² / (2 · (F_max/m - |g|))

Calculates the exact altitude required for maximum retro-propulsive thrust (126.0 m/s²) against gravity (45.0 m/s²) to reach zero vertical velocity at deck level.

02 • PPO clipped surrogate objective
L(θ) = E_t [ min(r_t · A_t, clip(r_t, 1±ε) · A_t) ]

Optimizes a Gaussian policy over two continuous throttle dimensions with variance clipping (ε = 0.2) to prevent policy collapse under severe angular perturbations.

03 • 9-Dimensional state tensor
s_t = [Δx/(W/2), Δy/(H/2), v_x/10, v_y/10, θ, ω, c_L, c_R, v_pad/10]

Normalized inputs describing vehicle position relative to the moving pad deck, attitude angle, angular rate, landing gear contact flags, and the ship's drift velocity.

04 • Potential-based reward shaping
R = ΔΦ - 0.20·u_main - 0.05·|u_rcs| - 0.10

Where Φ(s) = -100·√(dx²+dy²) - 100·√(vx²+vy²) - 100·|θ|. Enforces rapid descent via -0.10 step penalty while preserving optimal policy invariance.
