Mechanical Dynamics

The rocking-block touchdown mechanics

Orbital booster recoveries are not point-mass collisions. Structural stability is governed by non-linear kinetic restitution, foot-planted pivot torque, and geometric tipping boundaries.

Figure 1: Stability envelope & pivot dynamics θ_tip = 25.6°

Kinetic impact restitution (v ≤ 63.0 m/s)

Touchdown energy dissipation is modeled with vertical damping restitution e = 0.4 and tangential friction damping, reproducing authentic structural rebound and ground absorption before the gear settles flat.

Geometric stability boundary (θ_tip = 25.6°)

Derived directly from the vehicle's structural baseline: 22m landing gear footprint and 23m center-of-mass height. Beyond 25.6°, the center of gravity falls outside the contact footprint, producing an irreversible overturning moment under gravity.

Dynamic foot pivoting & cold-gas RCS authority

When the initial leg makes contact, the booster enters a rocking-block state, pivoting about that foot under gravity torque. The agent's RCS thrusters retain full control authority to restore verticality before the settling timer expires.

Mathematical Specifications

Guidance laws & policy formulations

Analytical equations governing the hoverslam ignition threshold, continuous policy optimization, state tensor representation, and potential reward invariance.

01 • Suicide burn ignition altitude
h_burn = v_y² / [2(F_max/m - |g|)]

Calculates the exact analytical altitude threshold for retro-propulsive ignition. Decelerates the booster under maximum continuous thrust (126.0 m/s²) against gravity (45.0 m/s²) so vertical velocity reaches zero at the precise moment of deck contact.

02 • PPO clipped surrogate objective
L(θ) = E_t[min(r_t·A_t, clip(r_t, 1±ε)·A_t)]

Optimizes a continuous bivariate Gaussian policy over main engine throttle and RCS attitude thrusters. First-order clipped surrogate bounds (ε = 0.20) prevent destructive policy collapse during aggressive high-angle corrections.

03 • 9-Dimensional state tensor
s_t = [Δx/(W/2), Δy/(H/2), v_x/10, v_y/10, θ, ω, c_L, c_R, v_pad/10]ᵀ

Normalized Markov state vector fed to the actor-critic network, comprising relative booster coordinates, linear velocities, pitch attitude, angular rate, bilateral gear contact sensors, and drone ship drift velocity (s_t ∈ ℝ9).

04 • Potential-based reward shaping
R_t = ΔΦ(s) - 0.20·u_main - 0.05·|u_rcs| - 0.10

Potential difference ΔΦ(s) = γΦ(st+1) - Φ(st) guarantees policy invariance (Ng et al., 1999) while guiding exploration toward the moving deck. The -0.10 step penalty forces the agent to execute a fuel-optimal, minimum-duration trajectory.
